## Choices

- `viewer/pages/assistant-popup.js`: use a `MutationObserver` on `#chat`, coalesced with `requestAnimationFrame`, for page-content changes.
- `viewer/pages/nav.js`: keep the availability poll active but skip requests while the pop-up exists; reuse the stylesheet when recreating the pop-up.
- `viewer/test/app-assistant-popup.spec.js`: inject a context item into the real chat response to isolate the unread regression, and advance the browser clock for availability polling.

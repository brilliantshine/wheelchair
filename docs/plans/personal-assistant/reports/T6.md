# T6 — Assistant in Settings, Threads and thread pages

## Built
- `viewer/pages/settings.js` (~557, in the Threads box): "Personal assistant" Off/On control (`#assistant-setting`) with the agreed line, saving `assistant` via `modeGroup` (the same two-value control as Tool steps).
- `viewer/pages/threads.js`: `assistantRow()` and `loadPreview()`. When a list item has `assistant: true` it is drawn first (not in Archived view), outside every project group and excluded from grouping; title "Personal assistant", link `/wheelchair/app/thread?id=<id>`, "N needs you" badge from the features data, a one-line preview of its latest reply (read from its `/chat`; default text "Ask it about your threads."), no archive control. Nothing is drawn when no item is marked.
- `viewer/pages/thread.js`: for `thread.assistant`: title "Personal assistant", meta "Across your projects", no lead chip / PR / Agents / Export / Archive controls, "Your copy" panel hidden and not loaded, aside controls not updated (plus CSS hides them), reply author shows "Assistant". `rememberSeen()` stores the newest shown item's `at` (ISO, only moves forward) in `localStorage` `wc-assistant-seen`. All pages: `by: "assistant"` bubble gets "From your assistant"; `answered_by: "assistant"` appends "· by your assistant" to the answered tag; `archived_by: "assistant"` makes the head badge "Archived by your assistant".
- `viewer/pages/thread.css`: assistant-thread aside hiding and `.by-assistant` label style.
- `viewer/test/app-assistant.spec.js`: 5 tests (rows at 390 and 1280 wide, on and off; assistant page; archived_by; Settings toggle).

## Choices
- Preview line is fetched from the assistant thread's `/chat` in `threads.js` (the list payload has no reply text); first non-empty line, markdown marks stripped, cut at 90 characters.
- Reply author on the assistant page reads "Assistant" (mockup), not the lead's name.
- Settings control sits in the "Threads" section of Models and preferences.
- Kept "Open in terminal" on the assistant page (Restart/dead-lead box unchanged).
- Aside controls hidden by not calling `WCAside.update` plus CSS in `thread.css`, since `aside.js` is outside my boundary.
- Read the assistant flag on the thread page from `thread.assistant` in the `/chat` payload.

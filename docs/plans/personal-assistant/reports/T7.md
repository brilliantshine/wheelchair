## Choices
- Button is the mockup's "✦ Assistant" pill (brief said round; the mockup's `border-radius:999px` pill wins), hidden while the panel is open.
- Side panel at ≥900px is the mockup's floating 410px bottom-right panel, no shade.
- Unread counts only assistant items and cards (not the user's own messages); an unset key counts as unread when the assistant has said anything.
- Closed-state polling every 10 s (open 2 s) in `assistant-popup.js`, so the dot updates without a page reload.
- Unanswered needs/other cards link to the thread instead of offering actions; question cards offer option buttons only.
- Escape and the shade close the panel (`assistant-popup.js`).

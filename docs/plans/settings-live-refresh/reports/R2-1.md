## Not covered (outside this brief, not tested)

The template editor's two checkboxes ("Saves after every step", "Runs the whole test suite") have no `data-focus` or id, so `swapKeepingFocus()` matches them by `aria-label`, which every template shares. Its selects and fields (`#trole`, `#tscope`, `#tpackages`, `#tunsettled`, `#tfiles`, `#tname`, `#textra`) are matched by shared id. From reading the code, any of these that does not hold back the repaint could pass focus to the replacement template in the same way. I did not change or test them.

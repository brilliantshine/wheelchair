Files changed: `machine/lib/harness-install.js`, `machine/test/harness-install.test.js`, `docs/plans/easy-onboarding/reports/R1-1.md`.

- `machine/test/harness-install.test.js`: added a malformed-version case to verify the specified no-working-node message.

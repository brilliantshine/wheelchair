# R7 — Wide mockup comment box

- `viewer/pages/picture.js`: put the mockup and comment panel in a shared content-width container without changing the tap layer.
- `viewer/pages/picture.css`: make the panel sticky within that container and size it to the pane, capped by its existing maximum width. Plain sticky on the original block cannot travel beyond its pane-width containing block.
- `viewer/test/app-spot-comment.spec.js`: verify visible controls stay within the pane after tapping and after scrolling left by 200px.

Screenshot after tapping at 390×844: `/tmp/wc-r7-11/picture.png`.

## Choices

- `picture.js` and `picture.css`: use a content-width wrapper and a pane-width sticky panel, preserving the existing 480px maximum.

# T3 report: hub route and the Settings Diagrams section

## What changed
- `viewer/app/diagram-sensitivity.js` (new): `read` and `write` fan out to every machine in parallel through `hub.call`, 5 s each (`WHEELCHAIR_DIAGRAM_TIMEOUT_MS` overrides it, for tests).
- `viewer/app/routes.js`: `GET`/`POST /settings/diagram-sensitivity`, placed at the top of `handleSettings`; `GET /settings` is untouched.
- `viewer/app/API.md`: both routes documented in the settings table.
- `viewer/pages/settings.js`: `api()` now sets `error.status` and `error.body` (message unchanged); a "Diagrams" section after "Threads"; choosing a level POSTs on every click, then re-reads.
- `viewer/pages/settings.css`: `.diagram-radios`/`.choice`/`.diagram-lines`, and `.note { white-space: pre-line }` so a multi-line failure note shows one line per machine.
- `viewer/test/app-diagram-sensitivity.test.js` (new, 4 tests) and `viewer/test/app-diagram-sensitivity.spec.js` (new, 5 specs x 2 browsers).

## Choices
- `viewer/app/diagram-sensitivity.js`: a machine's 4xx gives its error text; a 5xx, timeout, or refused connection gives "did not answer".
- `viewer/app/diagram-sensitivity.js`: the timeout is injected by the env var `WHEELCHAIR_DIAGRAM_TIMEOUT_MS`, because the test hub is a child process.
- `viewer/app/diagram-sensitivity.js`: a GET answer with a missing `level` is passed as `null` and a missing `files` as `[]`.
- `viewer/pages/settings.js`: the three radios are full-width button rows (name plus one-line hint), not the page's segmented control, so each line fits on a phone.
- `viewer/pages/settings.js`: the per-machine disagreement lines sit in their own `.diagram-lines` note under the choices; `setNote` (the section's top note) carries only the save and failure messages.
- `viewer/pages/settings.js`: the section reads when the Models tab is shown (once per visit), not on every re-render; leaving the tab resets it.
- `viewer/pages/settings.js`: if the hub itself cannot be reached, the section shows `hub: <error>` as the note and selects nothing.
- `viewer/test/app-diagram-sensitivity.spec.js`: browser specs stub the hub route with `page.route`; the hub's real fan-out is covered by the node tests.

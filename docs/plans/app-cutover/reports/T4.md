## API.md sentence for `brief`
"`brief` (the aside as the chat payload and the create route return it) is the aside without `history`, plus `graphs: [{entry, path}]`, one per history entry that has a `graph`, where `entry` is that entry's index in the aside's `history` (the `entry` the picture page takes) and `path` is the saved graph file."

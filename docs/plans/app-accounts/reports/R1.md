# R1 — hub remediation

## Changes

- `viewer/app/sessions.js`: suppress reports during future limit holds, record the waiting provider, and initialize role template and lead style on promotion.
- `viewer/app/usage.js`: preserve an unexpired account limit across newer ordinary readings.
- `viewer/app/settings.js`, `viewer/app/routes.js`: persist retired account IDs and exclude them when assigning new ones.
- `viewer/app/balancer.js`, `viewer/app/API.md`: select and document the provider shown while waiting.
- `viewer/test/app-balancer.test.js`, `viewer/test/app-usage.test.js`: cover the five remediation cases.

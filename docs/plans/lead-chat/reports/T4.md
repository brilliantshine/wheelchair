## Choices
- Spec seeds cards directly into the thread file and intercepts the press request, since the hub's `reach` kind may not exist yet (`app-reach.spec.js`).
- Eyebrow text "Talk to another thread" (brief did not name one) (`thread.js`).
- Buttons are plain `.actions` buttons, not full-width `.opt` blocks (`thread.js`).
- On `allow_failed`, neither button is marked picked and the tag uses the `resolved` style (`thread.js`).
- Not added to pinned or folded-strip lists: those are for pinned kinds only.

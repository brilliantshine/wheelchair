# T3 report: live screen check (attempt 2)

Attempt 1 (`5887030`, `1212f54`, cherry-picked from `w10`) is the base; this attempt tightens it.

## Changed
- `machine/test/screen-live.js`:
  - `cgroupPids` returns `null` when it cannot read (no unit, no ControlGroup, read error), not `[]`. It reads the unit's whole cgroup subtree, because a slice keeps its processes in child scopes and its own `cgroup.procs` is empty.
  - Slice check before stop: the screen slice itself (subtree only, no app-scope union) must be readable with at least one pid.
  - After stop, all checks poll up to 10 s (`TEARDOWN_MS`) before failing: the child is gone; the slice is gone (`LoadState` `not-found` or `ActiveState` `inactive`) or its subtree was read and is empty; no pid from the screen survives. The output names state the budget.
  - Focus check still asserts `run` of Chromium reports `focused` as its own window. On failure the line also carries `status` (from `screen status`) and `windows`.
  - Guards: a missing Chromium window skips the pointer and keyboard block with a failing check; a missing screen dir, `state.json` or app unit reads as empty; the xmessage argv check and the child check tolerate a `null` unit. The script always reaches cleanup and the summary.
  - Comment before `service.listen()` on the startup line `machine: screen startup cleanup failed: wcscreen-<s>.slice`: expected, because the parent slice does not exist yet.
- `machine/test/screen-live.sh`: `xdpyinfo` removed from the required tools.
- No screen code, `./install.sh` or live units touched. Nothing `rm`'d, no process killed, displays `:99`/`:100` untouched.

## Observations
- The startup line `machine: screen startup cleanup failed: wcscreen-<s>.slice` appears on every run and is expected (the parent slice does not exist yet).
- The focus failure in run 1 is the transient-window case the brief names; it is not a screen-code defect I could confirm here.

## Choices
- `cgroupPids` reads the whole cgroup subtree, not just the unit's own `cgroup.procs`: a slice's processes live in child scopes, so the slice's own file is always empty. Affects the slice check and the survivor and child checks in `screen-live.js`.
- "Slice gone" means `LoadState` `not-found` or `ActiveState` `inactive`, or its subtree read and empty; checked in `sliceGone` in `screen-live.js`.
- Teardown wait budget 10 s (`TEARDOWN_MS`), polled every 250 ms, applied to the three after-stop checks in `screen-live.js`.
- The survivor check still uses the union of slice and app-scope pids captured before stop (from attempt 1); the slice check itself uses only the slice subtree.
- Focus-failure detail adds `status` from `screen status` to the `windows` output, in `screen-live.js`.
- Guards: a missing Chromium window skips the pointer and keyboard block with a failing check; missing screen dir, `state.json` or app unit reads as empty. In `screen-live.js`.
- The startup-cleanup comment is placed in `screen-live.js` before `service.listen()`, not in `screen-live.sh`.
- `xdpyinfo` removed from the required tools in `screen-live.sh`.

## Observations
- Earlier iteration (before the script was finished): the first cold Chromium `run` returned `focused: null` once (cold start over the 5 s auto-focus window, Spec'd behaviour), then 22/22 on three consecutive runs. A slow machine can make that check flaky; not a screen-code bug.
- The script's first attempts failed from my own bugs (a blocking `spawnSync` stalled the in-process service; `focused` is `{id,name}`, not a number; an unset env var for the shot path). No screen-code defect found.

## Choices
- Session is started via the hub route `POST /v1/sessions` with a fake tmux whose `list-sessions` prints the new session's name afterwards.
- Page title `clicks=N len=M`; click lands at the window's centre-top third; Chromium launched with `--window-position=0,0 --window-size=900,600`.
- The no-cookie check uses `xwininfo -root` with `XAUTHORITY=/dev/null`, plus a positive control with the cookie.
- Shot path comes from `LIVE_SHOT` or `$TMPDIR/screen-live-shot.png`.
- Extra checks beyond the bullet: positive cookie control, child alive before stop, slice non-empty before stop, no surviving processes.
- The `xmessage` `$HOME` check reads `/proc/<pid>/cmdline` of processes in the app scope's cgroup.

- `viewer/app/sessions.js`: reopen a finished designer when its starter’s session message is delivered.
- `viewer/test/app-designer-revision.test.js`: cover reopening, status and result debt, sender isolation, and mapper behavior.
- `docs/plans/design-in-threads/reports/R2.md`: this report.

# T3 — hub activity and context

- `viewer/app/sessions.js`: stores steps, pending ends, turn boundaries, counts and context; closes steps at turn and session boundaries without delivering messages for activity events.
- `viewer/app/step-history.js`: project-specific clean timing history, cached lookups, median and pruning.
- `viewer/app/agents.js`: exposes activity, context and normal timing on agent nodes.
- `viewer/app/hub.js`: prunes at startup and daily.
- `viewer/test/app-activity.test.js`: covers pairing, closure upgrades, retention, concurrent steps, thinking, context and history.
- `viewer/app/API.md`, `viewer/AGENTS.md`: documents the new contract and module.

## Choices

- `viewer/app/step-history.js`: serialize appends and pruning per file to avoid losing samples during rewrites; cache per absolute history filename.
- `viewer/app/sessions.js`: expire pending ends on the next step event, and use receipt time for their expiry and cap ordering.

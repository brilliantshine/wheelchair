Suggested rules
Logins, secrets, payments: treat as critical automatically.
Changes that delete or migrate data: heavy at least, and ask before it runs.
Tests: written by the other family from the one that wrote the code, so they don't share its blind spots.
Work across many files: never the light tier. Light models lose track over long context.
Parts that come with your screenshots: a model that can read images.

## Adapter boundary

`index.js` statically registers five adapters and describes a smaller interface than the actual one (`machine/adapters/index.js:2-5`). All five expose `provider`, `loginEnv`, `seed`, `loginCommand`, `parseLogin`, `loginStatus`, `command`, `firstMessage`, `readyText`, `answerPrompts`, `findTranscript`, `hookEvent`, and `usageFrom` (`machine/adapters/claude.js:46`, `machine/adapters/codex.js:104`, `machine/adapters/agy.js:9`, `machine/adapters/kimi.js:158`, `machine/adapters/pi.js:17`). Service seeds non-default homes, optionally calls `prepare`, builds environment, launches `command`, sends `firstMessage` once `readyText` accepts the pane, and calls `answerPrompts` (`machine/service.js:803-812,831,1037-1045,1218`). It translates hooks and optionally runs `onTurnEnded`; `usageFrom` reads usage-source payloads (`machine/service.js:1617-1644`).

Optional service-facing members are `prepare`, `sessionNameEnv`, `promptText`, `retryStartupEnter`, `onTurnEnded`, `loginCwd`, `loginPrompt`, `loginDone`, and `codeNeedsPrompt` (`machine/service.js:812,831,1253-1255,1303,1624,1747,1783-1788,2311`). `usual` is used in live switching, which accepts only Claude and Codex (`machine/service.js:870,892-895`); `usageProbe` is required by the allowlisted usage endpoint for Claude, Codex, and Kimi (`machine/service.js:2282-2286`). `asideArgv` is used for Claude, Codex and Pi through a separate provider mapping (`machine/lib/aside-turns.js:61,95,123`). Pi and Kimi expose `resolveCli`; Pi also exposes `redact` and `spend` (`machine/service.js:1740,1832,1856,2274`). Adapter-specific exports include parse helpers and Codex override helpers (`machine/adapters/claude.js:46-49`, `machine/adapters/codex.js:104-111`, `machine/adapters/kimi.js:158-160`). The contract is behavioral rather than enforced by a common validator.

## End of turn and transcript

- Claude installs hooks in its command; Stop / StopFailure translate to end / failure and its session UUID finds the JSONL under the account projects directory (`machine/adapters/claude.js:57-73,87-103`).
- Codex notify supplies the turn completion. The adapter binds the thread only after a role-marker match, searches its account sessions tree and scans complete rollout lines for usage (`machine/adapters/codex.js:114-130,133-152`).
- Antigravity writes a Stop hook and uses its matching conversation event to bind the source transcript (`machine/adapters/agy.js:11-30,75-89`).
- Kimi has no translated hook: it binds its wire log, polls main-agent `turn.ended` rows with persisted offsets and spool gating, and terminates on a missing or replaced log (`machine/adapters/kimi.js:109-155,184`; `machine/service.js:1076-1162,2443`).
- Pi's extension posts `agent_settled` and tool events; its adapter translates those, and service copies the transcript before completing the event, redacting keys (`machine/adapters/pi-extension.js:2-20`; `machine/adapters/pi.js:75-93`; `machine/service.js:1489,1618-1622`).

The shell bridge handles Claude/Pi stdin, Codex last argument and Antigravity stdin/session environment separately (`machine/hook.sh:15-33`). The copier preserves complete lines and has Kimi file-identity checks (`machine/service.js:1478-1489`). Chat chooses five separate parsers: Claude human / queued attachments; Codex turn contexts and response items; Antigravity planner and generic rows; Kimi prompt and loop events; Pi message/tool-result rows (`viewer/app/chat.js:149-180,185-267,301-304`). Kimi reports select a turn's last text step; Pi reports require an assistant entry ID; the other three use their respective last-assistant row (`viewer/app/chat.js:270-299,369-375`; `viewer/app/sessions.js:435-450`).
